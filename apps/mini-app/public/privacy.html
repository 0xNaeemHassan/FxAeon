Privacy

This page describes data handling in the FxAeon app and landing page.

On your device

The app stores your appearance and transaction settings, selected wallet preferences, price caches, draft inputs, and transaction recovery records in browser storage. These records help restore your workspace; they do not authorize transactions. The landing page stores your chosen light or dark appearance on this device. Clearing site data removes these local records.

Wallets and sign-in

Connecting a wallet shares its public address and selected network with the app. Signing in through Privy may share your email or Telegram account information with Privy, depending on the method you choose. Privy manages authentication storage and embedded-wallet services. FxAeon does not ask you to enter a seed phrase or private key.

Disconnecting stops the app from using the selected wallet. It does not delete public blockchain records or revoke token approvals.

External services

The app requests balances, positions, transaction status, and market prices from blockchain and data providers. These requests can include your public wallet address and transaction hashes. Hosting and service providers receive network information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them. Transactions and wallet activity on public blockchains are publicly visible.

Read the policies of Privy, Alchemy, Cloudflare, and Telegram, and the wallet provider you use, for their data practices.

Cookies and analytics

The standalone landing page does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. The app uses browser storage for its features and Privy session storage for sign-in. FxAeon does not add marketing analytics or advertising trackers.
